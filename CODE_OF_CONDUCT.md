# Code of Conduct

We welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels. Be
respectful, explain technical disagreements constructively, and give others
room to learn.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, unwanted personal attention, and
publication of private information are not acceptable in project issues,
pull requests, reviews, or other project spaces.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer at
ramirez.ruiz.eliezer.reuven@gmail.com. Reports will be handled with care for the
privacy of those involved. The maintainer may remove harmful content or limit
participation to protect the community.
